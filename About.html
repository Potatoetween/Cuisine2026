Food

Food is any edible substance consumed to provide nutritional support, energy, and maintenance for living organisms

Food is essential because it provides the vital energy and nutrients our bodies need to survive, grow, and stay healthy

Zambian cuisine

Zambian cuisine is the traditional food and cooking styles of Zambia's different communities. It includes foods such as nshima, a thick maize-meal porridge, ifisashi (vegetables cooked in groundnut sauce), and chikanda (a traditional dish made from wild orchid tubers). Zambian foods provide energy and nutrients that help people grow, stay healthy, and remain active.

Nshima is the national staple food of Zambia, made from finely ground white cornmeal called mealie meal cooked with water into a thick, smooth, starchy porridge

Cooking nshima requires white cornmeal (mealie meal), water, and a steady stirring technique to achieve a smooth, thick consistency without lumps
